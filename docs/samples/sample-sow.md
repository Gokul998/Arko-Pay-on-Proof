# Statement of Work — Brightleaf Clinic Booking App (MVP)

**Client:** Brightleaf Clinics Ltd. (contact: Priya Nair, Head of Product)
**Contractor:** Lucas Moreau, independent full-stack developer
**Effective date:** on signature · **Total fee:** USD 6,000 (fixed price)

## 1. Scope
Build a web-based appointment booking MVP for three clinic locations: patients pick a clinic, doctor and time slot; staff manage availability; confirmation emails are sent automatically.

## 2. Phases, deliverables and acceptance

| Phase | Deliverables | Acceptance criteria | Due | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery & UX | Clickable Figma prototype (patient + staff flows), data model diagram (PDF) | Prototype covers booking, rescheduling, cancellation and staff availability; reviewed in one walkthrough call | 10 days after start | 30% |
| 2. Build | Deployed staging URL, GitHub repository, API documentation (OpenAPI) | All flows from the prototype work on staging; automated tests pass in CI; Lighthouse accessibility score ≥ 90 | 25 days after Phase 1 approval | 40% |
| 3. Launch & handover | Production deployment, admin guide (PDF), 30-minute recorded handover video | Production live on the client's domain; 3 staff accounts created; zero open P1 bugs after 5 business days | 10 days after Phase 2 approval | 30% |

## 3. Terms
- Each phase is reviewed by the client within **5 business days** of delivery.
- Up to **2 revision rounds** per phase are included.
- Delays caused by missing client inputs extend the affected due date by the same number of days.
- Either party may cancel unstarted phases with full refund of any amounts held for them.
- Source code and design files are transferred to the client on final payment.
